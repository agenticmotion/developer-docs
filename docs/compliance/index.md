# AZTA Compliance

Technical control mappings between AZTA and established AI governance and security frameworks.

## Frameworks

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023
- EU AI Act
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- OWASP guidance for agentic applications

These mappings describe technical relationships between AZTA controls and applicable framework requirements. They are not, by themselves, claims of regulatory certification or legal compliance.
